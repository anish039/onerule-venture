Our Story

Built by Entrepreneurs,
for Entrepreneurs

We started OneRule Venture because we experienced firsthand how hard it is to build a business in India without the right intelligence, mentorship, and tools.

Mission

Democratising Business Intelligence for Every Indian Entrepreneur

For too long, quality business data, strategic guidance, and funding networks were locked behind expensive consultants and elite networks accessible only to a privileged few.

OneRule Venture was built to change that. We believe every entrepreneur — whether in Mumbai or Madurai, with ₹10,000 or ₹10 crore — deserves access to world-class business intelligence to make smarter decisions faster.

Our Journey

From Idea to India's Trusted Entrepreneur Platform

Every milestone has been driven by one thing: making business smarter for every Indian entrepreneur.

2021

The Idea is Born

Founders identify a critical gap — Indian entrepreneurs lack affordable, real-time business intelligence. OneRule Venture is incorporated in Bengaluru.

2022

Beta Launch & First 500 Members

Platform launches in private beta with core business health tools and government scheme tracker. Seed funding of ₹3.5 Cr raised from angel investors.

2023

Series A & AI Integration

₹24 Cr Series A raised. AI-powered insights engine launched. Crossed 5,000 active users. Expanded to 10 major cities with a mentor network of 200+.

2024

National Scale & 12,000+ Members

Operations expanded to all 28 states. Industry reports library crossed 340 titles. Partnership with SIDBI and NASSCOM formalized. 95% satisfaction score.

2025

The Next Chapter

Launching advanced AI co-pilot for business owners, expanding into Southeast Asia markets, and working towards the 1 million businesses mission.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Radical Transparency

We share data, methodology, and limitations openly. No black-box insights — you always know how we arrived at a recommendation.

Entrepreneur First

Every product decision starts with one question: does this genuinely help an entrepreneur grow? Not engagement metrics, not virality.

India-Rooted

Our insights are built for Indian markets, regulations, culture, and consumer behaviour — not adapted from Western playbooks.

Continuous Learning

Markets change, and so do we. Our team runs weekly research cycles to keep every insight, report, and tool current and relevant.
