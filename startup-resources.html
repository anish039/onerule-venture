Run customer discovery interviews, validate the problem, and assess market size using our Market Opportunity Checker tool before spending a rupee.

Legal Setup

Register Your Business in India

A simplified guide to getting your startup legally incorporated and compliant from Day 1.

Choose Legal Structure

Private Limited Company is recommended for VC-backed startups. LLP works for service businesses. Sole Proprietorship for early-stage solopreneurs.

⏱ Time: 1–2 days to decide  |  Cost: Free

MCA Incorporation

File SPICe+ form on the MCA21 portal for Pvt. Ltd. registration. Get Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, TAN, and GSTIN in a single application.

⏱ Time: 7–14 days  |  Cost: ₹5,000–15,000

DPIIT Recognition

Register on the Startup India portal for DPIIT recognition. Unlocks tax benefits (3-year exemption), easier compliance, and access to 100+ government schemes.

⏱ Time: 2–7 days  |  Cost: Free

GST Registration

Mandatory if annual turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh in special states). Register on the GST portal and file returns monthly or quarterly.

⏱ Time: 3–7 days  |  Cost: Free

IP Protection

File trademark application (₹4,500–9,000) on IP India portal to protect your brand name. Consider patents for technical inventions. Copyright is automatic for software.

⏱ Time: 18–24 months  |  Start early

Open Business Bank Account

Open a current account with ICICI, HDFC, or RazorpayX using your incorporation documents. Maintain separate finances from Day 1 — it's non-negotiable.

⏱ Time: 1–3 days  |  Free to open
